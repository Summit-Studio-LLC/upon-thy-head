Four directions, each shown at the same four moments: recording, just after amen, the timeline, and reading a blessing with polish edits visible. The sample blessing and names are placeholders.

Dew

Light comes from above and rests on the person receiving it, after the promise that truth will distil “as the dews from heaven.” Pale morning colors, one typeface, almost nothing on screen.

Risk: it is so quiet that the timeline could pass for a well-made notes app.

Firmament

Each blessing becomes a light in a sky that belongs to one person. The timeline is a star chart drawn to scale, with a month ruler down the edge, so the long gaps and the close clusters of a life are visible.

Risk: night skies drift toward horoscope apps. The engraved-atlas restraint is what holds it back, and it has to stay restrained.

Olive branch

Built from the materials of the ordinance: consecrated olive oil, olive wood, white cloth. The recording light is warm like oil catching a lamp, and every blessing adds a leaf to a branch that grows through the years.

Risk: the branch needs real illustration care. Drawn carelessly it turns into decoration.

The typed page

A patriarchal blessing is a typed sheet kept in a folder and read for a lifetime. This direction makes every blessing that same object: a page in a folder, with polish edits as pencil marks and a slip of paper showing the original.

Risk: typewriter type reads slower than a book face. Blessings are short, so it likely holds, but it is the least celestial of the four.
