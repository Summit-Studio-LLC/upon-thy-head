# Round 5 icon concepts

Created with the built-in image generation tool, one raster generation per concept/attempt. No SVG or code-drawn icon geometry. Inspected only the five supplied reference images; round-4 was ignored. K7 was the sole image reference passed for crown generations. Hands were generated without a visual reference to avoid importing spherical shading.

## Recommendations and ranking

Hands: **F5 first, F9 second**. Recommend **F5**: the back-of-head and shoulders make the blessing scene most explicit and keep the disc from becoming a floating ball. F9 is the strongest disc-only pair because its asymmetry feels more like a particular gesture.

Hands ranking: F5, F9, F3, F2, F7, F10, F8, F1, F6, F4.

Crowns: **G2 first, G1 second**. Recommend **G2**: it preserves the chosen K7 mood and gives the arch a little more visual weight, with restrained node light. G1 is the closest quiet continuation of K7. Among the fully flat alternatives, **G10** is the strongest small-size option.

Crown ranking: G2, G1, G4, G3, G10, G12, G6, G11, G9, G8, G7, G5.

## Inspection and limits

All 22 individual assets are 1024 x 1024, square full bleed. All ten hands and G9–G12 were visually checked as flat drawings before thresholding; no selected source has shaded three-dimensional modelling. Thresholding removes raster antialiasing and faint near-white/near-black raster variation, not volume. Their final PNGs contain exactly two pixel values, pure black and pure white. Resizing and centering place the visible marks within the central 70% (soft glow can have a faint fringe beyond the main form). A 2% black threshold removes near-black raster speckle from the soft crown backgrounds while retaining the allowed soft sphere and arch tones. No icon paths or contours were drawn in code.

The sheets have mid-grey grounds, 400px large tiles, names, and actual-size rounded previews. Reduced previews are antialiased and consequently have greys; the source flat PNGs do not. Rounded preview masks and labels are sheet furniture only.

Hands remain less distinctive at 29px. F4's mitten abstraction is not fully solved after three attempts; flipper/mitten readings remain. F6 is uniform outline but weak at 29px. Symmetric pairs F1–F3 can still read as brow-like shapes when finger detail vanishes; F5 and F9 are preferable. No selected large mark primarily reads as a moustache, lifted hat, antlers, butterfly or bird.

The fully flat crown row was regenerated after first-sheet inspection to strengthen the arch at 29px. The line and nodes remain connected in the 1024px originals; tiny tapered tips inevitably fade under downsampling. G10 carries the clearest line. G12's enlargement is modest in the final selected version; generation did not follow numerical geometry literally. No deterministic redrawing was used to force it.

## Attempts
