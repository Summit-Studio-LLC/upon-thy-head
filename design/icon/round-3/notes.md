# Round 3 — soft light on black

All 14 concepts were made with the built-in image generation tool using local raster references. Final PNGs are 1024 × 1024, full square. ImageMagick only resized the generated artwork and assembled the labelled contact sheet, including presentation-only rounded masks. No concept artwork was drawn with code.

## Recommendation

**K2.** Its slightly heavier arch and larger lights keep the connection visible at 29 px while preserving a softly modelled sphere and clear black separation. K1 is the closest continuation of the owner's favourite and the quieter alternative.

## Crown ranking

1. **K2** — Best small-size balance: visible arch, distinct lights and a readable sphere.
2. **K1** — Calmest direct refinement of the favourite; finer arch becomes delicate at 29 px.
3. **K5** — Uneven spacing adds a personal sense of time without adding shapes.
4. **K7** — Tapering bare arch ends feel gentle; their tips fade away when small.
5. **K6** — Wide sky over a larger, lower person; good separation but a more dispersed silhouette.
6. **K3** — Five lights clearly tell the accumulated-blessings story; busier than the three-light options.
7. **K4** — Bright central light creates hierarchy; unequal side lights lose their differences when tiny.
8. **K8** — Spacious and quiet, but the smaller sphere gives away useful small-size presence.

## Hands ranking

1. **H2** — Reduced rear head gives the clearest direct gesture with little extra anatomy.
2. **H6** — Asymmetric resting hands feel personal; the rear head remains recognisable when small.
3. **H1** — Tender and explicit from behind; shoulders add mass and compete at small sizes.
4. **H3** — Cleanest sphere-based hands option; hovering gap is delicate and can read as two marks at 29 px.
5. **H5** — Successfully joins hands and three-light arch at larger sizes; too many layers at 29 px.
6. **H4** — Overhead contact is clear at large size; diagonal hands become an ambiguous pale cluster at 29 px.

## Small-size assessment

Judged from raster previews at the actual pixel dimensions, not from enlarged icons. “Holds” describes visual recognition, not a user-testing result.

|---|---|---|

| K2 | Strong | Strongest crown; connection survives best |
| K3 | Holds; five distinct lights | Overall motif holds; individual light count less immediate |
| K4 | Holds; central hierarchy clear | Partial: tiny side light and size differences weaken |
| K5 | Holds; asymmetry visible | Holds overall; uneven timing becomes subtle |
| K6 | Holds; broad sky relationship | Holds overall; fine wide arch is faint |

| K8 | Holds, with smaller sphere | Partial: sphere is undersized beside other K choices |
| H1 | Holds: rear person under hands | Gesture survives; shoulders crowd the silhouette |
| H2 | Strong direct resting gesture | Best hands option; fingers merge but head and contact remain |
| H3 | Holds; small hovering gap visible | Partial: gap and fingers weaken; risks two marks over a ball |

| H5 | Both motifs readable, already busier | Does not hold both meanings cleanly |
| H6 | Strong direct resting gesture | Holds silhouette; finger detail and asymmetry diminish |

The overhead direction did not become a strong tiny icon within three tries. The first H4 read as a front-view ball, the second became too anatomical, and the selected third preserves the overhead composition with smooth simplified hands. H5 demonstrates the combined direction, but I would choose a single motif for the shipping icon.
