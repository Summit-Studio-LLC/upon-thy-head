      What to judge

      Whether four permission questions in a row feel like a gate or like setting up. Each is one screen, then iOS’s own question.

      There is no Skip on the permission screens: Apple’s review asks that a screen before a system question only say Continue. “Don’t Allow” in iOS’s question is the way to decline.

      Drawn as look-alikes

      The grey questions are drawings of iOS’s, with the app’s real lines from Info.plist. On iOS 26 the speech question may show Apple’s own wording instead (open question 32).
