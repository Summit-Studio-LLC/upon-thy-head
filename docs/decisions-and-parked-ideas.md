### 2026-11-21

- **Polish's light over the words (built 2026-11-21; Noah: "polishing should have a nice loading animation").** No spinner: the progress is shown on the blessing's own words on the review page (`lib/review/polish_light.dart`).
  - **While the model works:** the piece of the words with it (the same pieces of up to 1,200 characters it is sent in) carries a slow, soft band of gold light passing over it, a little downhill, behind the letters and warming them toward gold; between passes the piece keeps a faint steady glow. Pieces not yet reached are dimmed toward the page (36%); finished pieces are as always. Each change of piece fades over about a second. `Polish.runWithModel` and `ModelSource.find` now take `onPiece(index, pieces)`: each piece as it is sent, then `pieces.length` when all are answered; never after a cancel or with no model.
  - **The label:** a small gold light, drawn like a blessing's light on the sky, breathing four seconds in and six out (the sky's own breath), before "Polishing…". It grows with the text size, to twice its size at most.
  - **The finish:** the words polish changed (each change's original words) glint gold once, about 1.5 seconds rising and settling; the row reads "Polished for reading" at once; **the changes screen now opens when the glint has settled, about 1.5 seconds later than before** (it used to open at once and would have hidden the glint). Rules-only polish (older phones, Android) gets the glint and nothing else, with no waiting state. Nothing found: everything settles, no glint.
  - **Reduce Motion** (`MediaQuery.disableAnimations`, as elsewhere): no band and no breathing; the piece being worked on has a steady soft tint; the glint is a fade in and out. Those fades use `AnimationBehavior.preserve`, because Flutter otherwise cuts an animation to a twentieth of its length under the system's Reduce Motion and the fade was lost.
  - **How it is drawn:** it only paints, over and under the existing `CupertinoTextField`. It reads where the words are from the field's own laid-out text (`RenderEditable.getBoxesForSelection`, kept until the words or the width change), so it stays on the right words as the page scrolls, at any text size, and when words are corrected while the model works. It is its own repaint boundary over the field's kept picture; a frame of the band repaints that layer alone and lays out nothing. Every ticker stops on finish, cancel, leaving the page, and disposal. Editing, selection and the screen-reader node are untouched; the light adds nothing to be read; the live "Polishing…" announcement stays.
  - **Also fixed:** "Polishing…" and "Nothing to polish." sat in fixed 44-point boxes, which cut the words off at the largest text sizes; they are now at least 44 points.
  - **My choices (not ruled on):** the band's speed (a 4.2-second pass, then 1.4 seconds of rest), its brightness (17% gold behind, letters warmed up to about 60%), the 36% dimming, the light before the label rather than after, the 1.5-second glint and holding the changes screen for it, and glinting the original words of each change (the field shows the spoken words).
  - **Proven:** `test/polish_light_test.dart` (the pieces reported in order and then the end, none after a cancel or with no model; the band on pieces one, two and three in turn; every ticker stopped and no frame scheduled after finishing, leaving, and taking the page away; Reduce Motion with no moving band, no breathing and the glint still a full fade; rules-only with no waiting state; nothing to polish with no glint). On iPhone 17 Pro (clean) with a stand-in model (`integration_test/stand_in_model.dart`, under integration_test/ so no app build can reach it; about three seconds a piece on film so the walk's scrolling keeps up) by `tool/polish_light_walk.sh`: a three-piece blessing filmed from press to the changes screen, again with Reduce Motion, and once in Spanish at the largest text size (`design/sim/polish-light/`).
  - **Not proven:** the real model's pace (a few seconds a piece is expected). On this simulator, Reduce Motion written to its settings did not reach the app even after a restart; that run sets Flutter's own flag instead, as `reduce_motion_test.dart` does. Not seen on a phone or on Android.
  - **Seen in passing, not touched:** at the largest text size in Spanish the changes screen's "1 de 3" overlaps "Mayúscula" under "Cambio leve".

- **Polish with the phone's own model (built 2026-11-21, iPhone and iPad; Noah: use the phone's AI to polish better than rules can).** Apple's on-device model (Foundation Models, iOS 26, Apple Intelligence on) is a second source beside the rules. Android stays rules only.
  - **Only when Polish is pressed.** A correction afterwards runs the rules again, never the model. Rules and model both run; where both change the same words, the rules' change wins.
  - **The app does not trust the model** (`lib/polish/model_check.dart`). Its text is lined up word by word with the spoken words. Kept: punctuation, capitals, paragraph breaks (made a blank line), and one word for one word when it was plainly misheard (a sound-alike pair, a near spelling, accents) or is a name spelled as the blessing's details spell it. Dropped without a word: words added, left out or moved, a phrase for a phrase, numbers, names the details do not hold, a paragraph joined, markup, and any change to scripture language ("thee", "thou", "unto", "-eth"/"-est" forms; "vosotros", "os", "vuestro", the "-áis"/"-éis" forms). A piece where under 70% of the words come back the same is dropped whole.
  - **Sizes:** punctuation, capitals and paragraphs measure light; a corrected word moderate (the existing `measureSize`). Nothing the model can make measures heavy. Stored with the existing kinds `punctuation` and `grammar`, so files and the website opener need no change; the changes screen names them "Punctuation", "Capital letter", "New paragraph", "Word corrected".
  - **Long blessings** are cut into pieces of at most 1,200 characters at a paragraph, a sentence end, a comma, or between words, and each piece goes in a session of its own. Apple's model reads 4,096 tokens at once for instructions, piece and answer together (TN3193); 1,200 characters is about 400 tokens. Each piece has 25 seconds; a refused, failed or late piece gets the rules alone and the rest carries on.
  - **While it works** the Polish line reads "Polishing…". Leaving the page cancels the work and nothing is added. With no model (older phone, Apple Intelligence off, model not ready, language not taken, Android), polish is exactly as before; the hint under the button mentions punctuation only when the model is there.
  - **Nothing leaves the phone:** only `SystemLanguageModel` is used, with no tools. The framework's cloud model is a separate type the app never names. Nothing of a blessing is logged, not even in an error.
  - **The phone check** now reports the model's availability and reason (Speech engines item).
  - **My choices (not ruled on):** Apple's "permissive content transformations" guardrails, since blessings speak of illness and death; greedy sampling; the 70% and 25-second limits; capitals may be added anywhere but taken away only at a sentence start, never from "I" or a mid-sentence name; a paragraph already in the words is never joined; FoundationModels is weakly linked so iOS 17-25 phones still launch.

- **Proof of the app and warnings for the link service (built from the coordinator's brief; Noah: "let's add the attester for ios and android and add warning limits, and call it done").**
  - **What was built:**
    - **The service** (`website/lib/links/proof.ts`, `service.ts`, `store.ts`, `alerts.ts`; routes `app/api/proof/`, `app/api/proof/challenge/`, `app/api/status/`): a stateless five-minute challenge signed with `LINKS_PASS_SECRET`; `POST /api/proof` verifies an App Attest attestation (chain to Apple's App Attest root, nonce, key id, app id hash, zero counter, production environment) or a Play Integrity token (decoded by Google with a service account; package, requestHash, timestamp, `PLAY_RECOGNIZED`, `MEETS_DEVICE_INTEGRITY`) and answers with a seven-day pass, signed and kept nowhere. `POST /api/b` takes it in `Blessing-Pass`. A bad or expired pass is `401 pass`; no pass is still accepted unless `LINKS_REQUIRE_PROOF=1` (`403 proof`). Day counts with nothing about who: links with and without proof, proofs that verified and failed (by reason). `GET /api/status` (Bearer `CRON_SECRET`) shows today and yesterday. Warnings by Resend at 50% and 90% of the day's links or bytes, once each a day, sent after the answer (Next's `after()`). The full contract is in `website/PROTOCOL.md` §4, "Proof of the app".
    - **The app:** `lib/share/proof.dart` and the pass handling in `lib/share/link_service.dart`; native `ios/Runner/Proof.swift` (DCAppAttestService, pass in the Keychain on this device only) and `android/…/ProofChannel.kt` (Play Integrity standard request, pass in private preferences, which are never backed up). Entitlement `com.apple.developer.devicecheck.appattest-environment` = production. Gradle dependency `com.google.android.play:integrity:1.4.0`.
  - **The database:** one new table, `link_counts (day, what, n)`, made by the service with `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS` on first use, like the other two. Nothing existing changes. Swept with the day totals after three days.
  - **Claude's choices (open to change):**
    - The pass id comes from what was proven (the one-time App Attest key, or the challenge a Play token was bound to), not fresh randomness, so a replayed proof gives the same pass id and not another allowance. It is still kept nowhere.
    - A pass is limited per pass id (10 in 10 minutes, 40 a day) **as well as** the network's own limits, not instead of them.
    - The Android Cloud project number comes from the service with the challenge (`PLAY_INTEGRITY_PROJECT_NUMBER`), so setting Play Integrity up needs no new app build.
    - The pass is kept natively (Keychain / private preferences), so it outlives a launch and a phone attests about once a week, not at every launch.
    - The app gives proof at most 6 seconds, then makes the link without a pass. A pass with under an hour left is renewed before use.
    - The proof service runs only for the real https address; a debug build pointed at the website on this Mac sends no proof.

## Research: a downloadable speech model (2026-11-15, no code)

Why it matters: Apple's engines write Spanish on the device only once Apple's Spanish dictation asset is installed. A fresh simulator and a Mac without it report Spanish as server-only. A model the app downloads would make Spanish work on any recent phone, whatever Apple has installed. Both options below run OpenAI's Whisper models, which are released with their code under the MIT licence.

| | WhisperKit (Argmax) | whisper.cpp (ggml) |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Swift package; Whisper converted to Core ML, runs on the Neural Engine | C/C++ library; optional Core ML encoder for the Neural Engine, Metal for the rest |
| Licence | MIT for the open framework; Argmax's "Pro" SDK is under a separate commercial licence | MIT |
| Model sizes | tiny ~40 MB, base ~75 MB, small ~250 MB, large-v3 4-bit ~626 MB, large-v3-turbo 4-bit ~632 MB (*the first three unconfirmed; the 626/632 MB names are in Argmax's model repo*) | ggml files about the same as Whisper's (base ~140 MB, small ~460 MB in full precision, smaller quantised) (*unconfirmed*); the Core ML encoder is an extra download on top |
| Speed, recent iPhone | base: a 15-minute file in 51 s on an iPhone 15 Pro, release build (one write-up). Real-time factors on an iPhone 15 Pro of ~0.1 tiny, ~0.3 small, ~0.7 medium (*aggregator, unconfirmed*) | small.en Q5_1 on an iPhone 15 Pro: real-time factor ~0.4–0.5 (*community guide, unconfirmed*) |
| Speed, older iPhone | No primary figures found | small.en Q5_1 on an iPhone 11: ~0.8, "just keeps up" (*community guide, unconfirmed*) |
| Minimum iOS | iOS 17 in Argmax's own comparison; iOS 16 elsewhere (*conflicting*) | Core ML path iOS 15 or later |
| First run | Core ML models compile for the device on first use, which takes a while (*reported*) | Same for the Core ML encoder |

Spanish accuracy: large-v3-turbo is large-v3 with the decoder cut from 32 to 4 layers (1.55 B to 809 M parameters). Community scores on Common Voice 17 Spanish disagree (6.9 % and 15.4 % word error rate) because they normalise text differently. No official figure was found; *unconfirmed*.

- **Sharing as text and as a PDF (built 2026-11-16).**

  - The text is the title, the long date, "A blessing from …" and "For …" when known, a blank line, then the words in paragraphs. The words are the reading page's (polished when any change is kept). Labels and date follow the phone's language; the title, names and words never change. The share sheet's subject and header are the title (through LinkPresentation, a system framework).
  - The PDF is drawn natively with Core Text from the Newsreader fonts in the Flutter assets, at the app's settings (weight 400, optical size following the size). Quartz embeds each optical size as its own TrueType subset ("Newsreader16pt-Regular_wght_opsz…"), with MacRoman encoding, so its text is real, selectable and searchable without a ToUnicode table; Spanish accents are in MacRoman. A character outside MacRoman (a ligature, an emoji) would make Quartz use a different font type; not tested.
  - Paper: US Letter for the regions CLDR lists as Letter (US, Canada, Mexico, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Guatemala, Panama, Philippines, Puerto Rico, El Salvador, Venezuela, Nicaragua, Belize), A4 everywhere else. iOS has no paper-size setting of its own to read.
  - Pages: a one-inch margin, the words at 12.5 pt on 20.5 pt lines, half a line between paragraphs. Lines are laid out one by one, so a paragraph's first line is never left alone at the foot of a page and its last never alone at the head (a two-line paragraph moves whole). Footer from page 2: the title (cut with "…" if long) and "2 of 3".
  - The file lives in the app's tmp/share/ only while the sheet is up. It is removed when the sheet closes, when the app locks with the sheet up (the sheet is dismissed first), and at the next launch after the app was killed. The document's information holds only the title; iOS adds its own "Producer" (the iOS version and "Quartz PDFContext"). No app name.
  - The app switcher: the existing cover goes over the system share sheet too (checked natively and in a screenshot, `design/sim/share/05-en-switcher-with-sheet.png`).
